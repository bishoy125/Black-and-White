Website Usage Policy

Welcome to our website! By using our platform, you agree to abide by the following usage policy. Please read this policy carefully before using our services. Failure to follow the rules outlined in this policy may result in penalties, including being banned from the site.

1. Respectful Behavior

We expect all users to maintain a respectful and positive environment on our platform. Any form of abusive, offensive, or discriminatory language will not be tolerated. Repeated offenses will result in penalties.

2. Reporting Misconduct

If you encounter inappropriate behavior or content, you are encouraged to report it. After 5 valid reports accompanied by evidence (such as screenshots), the user involved will be reviewed, and if necessary, removed from the platform.

Evidence is critical in maintaining a fair environment. Therefore, please ensure you submit clear and relevant screenshots or other forms of proof when making a report.

3. No Tolerance for Spamming

Spam content, such as unsolicited advertisements, mass messaging, or links to harmful sites, is strictly prohibited. If spamming occurs, the user will be given a warning, and repeated offenses will lead to account suspension or permanent ban.

4. Usage of Content

All users must respect intellectual property and the rights of others. You may not copy, distribute, or exploit any content (including text, images, or media) without proper authorization. Any violation will result in immediate account suspension and potential legal action.

5. Account Security

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their accounts. Sharing your login details with others is not permitted. Any breach of security, such as account hacking, will result in a permanent ban from the site.

6. Consequences of Violating the Policy

Violating any of the above rules will result in penalties based on the severity of the violation. The penalties may include:

  • Warnings – A warning will be issued for minor violations.
  • Temporary Suspension – If violations continue, a temporary ban from the platform may be imposed.
  • Permanent Ban – Repeated violations or serious misconduct will result in permanent removal from the website.

7. Changes to the Usage Policy

We reserve the right to update this usage policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and it is your responsibility to stay informed of any modifications.
