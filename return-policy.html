Return Policy

1. Introduction

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If for any reason you are not happy with your item, we offer a return policy that allows you to return your items within a specific time frame for a refund or exchange. Please read the terms below to understand our return policy fully.

2. Return Eligibility

In order to qualify for a return, the following conditions must be met:

  • The item must be in its original condition, unused, and undamaged.
  • The item must be returned within 30 days from the date of purchase.
  • Items must be accompanied by the original receipt or proof of purchase.
  • Items marked as "final sale" or "non-returnable" are not eligible for return.

3. Return Process

To initiate a return, please follow these steps:

  1. Contact our customer service team at info@example.com or through our Contact Us page to request a return authorization.
  2. Once you receive a return authorization, pack the item securely in its original packaging (if possible).
  3. Send the item to the provided return address along with the return authorization number.
  4. We will process your return once the item is received and inspected.

4. Refunds and Exchanges

Once we receive your returned item and confirm that it meets the return conditions, we will issue your refund or exchange as follows:

  • Refunds will be credited back to the original payment method within 7-10 business days.
  • If you opt for an exchange, we will send you the replacement item once the returned item is processed.

5. Shipping Costs

Shipping costs are non-refundable. If you are returning an item for a refund, the cost of shipping will be deducted from the total refund amount. Return shipping is at the customer's expense unless the return is due to a defective or incorrect item.

6. Damaged or Defective Items

If your item arrives damaged or defective, please contact us immediately. We will provide a return label and offer a replacement or full refund, including shipping costs.

7. Non-Returnable Items

The following items are non-returnable and cannot be refunded or exchanged:

  • Gift cards
  • Personalized or custom-made items
  • Items purchased through clearance or sale events marked as non-returnable

8. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our return policy, please don't hesitate to contact us at info@example.com or visit our Contact Us page.
